Wednesday 11 November 2026

The wars dominated the day, but diplomacy made the headlines. President Donald Trump declared the Iran war "militarily effectively over" after meeting Qatar's premier, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio said US economic pressure had worked well enough that "there's really no need for major combat operations any longer" — though both warned force remained possible. The Justice Department charged three alleged Iran-backed operatives over nearly 20 plots in the US and Europe, including a planned attack on RAF Fairford, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was moved to a military base after threats. Iran said it would "never return" to its pre-war status, with the Strait of Hormuz shut 250+ days.

In Abu Dhabi, the first US-brokered Ukraine-Russia talks since February ended without a ceasefire but with agreement on a large prisoner swap, called by envoy Jared Kushner the biggest of the war. NATO jets scrambled after drones strayed into Baltic airspace. In Gaza, Israeli strikes killed at least eight people, including a father and his 13-year-old son, as the ceasefire frayed; Israel's newly sworn-in parliament has no government after a deadlocked election.

At COP31 in Antalya, UN chief António Guterres told 102 world leaders it was "make or bake time", warning the planet is heading for more than 2.5°C of warming even if current pledges are kept, and proposed a climate justice compact partly funded by taxes on oil companies.

October consumer prices rose 0.4%, hotter than expected, reviving December rate-rise talk, and House Republicans blocked impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, 211-204.

China's trade surplus passed $1 trillion in ten months, and Anthropic was reported to seek up to $3.5 billion in an IPO valuing it near $1 trillion. Nvidia chief Jensen Huang faced bipartisan calls for a federal probe and a senator's demand that he resign over alleged Chinese spy ties. OL Lyonnes beat Juventus 6-0 in the Women's Champions League.
